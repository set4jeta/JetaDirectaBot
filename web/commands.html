Two things worth knowing

English only, on purpose

Command names are English so the same bot works in any server. What changes with /language is what the bot replies, not how its commands are called.

Alerts arrive, you don't ask

The point of the bot is the alert: when a tracked pro queues up, the embed appears in your channel on its own. The commands here are for looking things up in between.

What if this came to you on its own?

LoLProTrackr posts this alert to your Discord channel —or your DMs— the moment a player enters a game, across the 20 leagues in the catalogue. Free.
