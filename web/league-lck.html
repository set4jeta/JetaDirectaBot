Frequently asked questions

Who has the highest SoloQ rank in the LCK?

Sharvel (DNS, Jungle), at Challenger · 2615 LP. It is the highest of the 55 LCK players that LoLProTrackr has indexed.

How many professional players does the LCK have?

55 players across 10 teams, with about 189 SoloQ accounts between them: an average of 3.4 accounts per player. It comes from counting the LCK leaderboard, not from an estimate.

Can you tell when a LCK player queues up for SoloQ?

Yes. LoLProTrackr posts to your Discord channel with the champion, the role, the rank and the team of the LCK player as soon as the game starts, without you having to ask for anything.

Which teams are in the LCK?

BNK FEARX (BFX), HANJIN BRION (BRO), Dplus Kia (DK), DN SOOPers (DNS), Gen.G (GENG), Hanwha Life Esports (HLE), Kiwoom DRX (KRX), KT Rolster (KT), KT Rolster Academy (NS), T1 (T1).

When is the next LCK match?

kt Rolster vs Dplus KIA, on Friday 4 September at 08:00 UTC (Playoffs). After that there are 4 more spread over 5 days of the schedule.

Where do the LCK SoloQ accounts come from?

From each player's known accounts, checked one by one against Riot's API on kr. For every player the highest-ranked account is published, not all of them: someone with four accounts has a single relevant rank, the one they compete on.
