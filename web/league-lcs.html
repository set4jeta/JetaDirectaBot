Frequently asked questions

Who has the highest SoloQ rank in the LCS?

Loki (C9, Mid), at Challenger · 3664 LP. It is the highest of the 40 LCS players that LoLProTrackr has indexed.

How many professional players does the LCS have?

42 players across 8 teams, with about 144 SoloQ accounts between them: an average of 3.4 accounts per player. It comes from counting the LCS leaderboard, not from an estimate.

Can you tell when a LCS player queues up for SoloQ?

Yes. LoLProTrackr posts to your Discord channel with the champion, the role, the rank and the team of the LCS player as soon as the game starts, without you having to ask for anything.

Which teams are in the LCS?

Cloud9 (C9), DIG (DIG), Disguised (DSG), FlyQuest (FLY), LYON (2024 American Team) (LYON), Sentinels (SEN), Shopify Rebellion (SR), Team Liquid (TLAW).

When is the next LCS match?

Sentinels vs Cloud9 Kia, on Saturday 5 September at 20:00 UTC (Week 7). After that there are 13 more spread over 12 days of the schedule.

Where do the LCS SoloQ accounts come from?

From each player's known accounts, checked one by one against Riot's API on na1. For every player the highest-ranked account is published, not all of them: someone with four accounts has a single relevant rank, the one they compete on.
