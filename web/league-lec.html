Frequently asked questions

Who has the highest SoloQ rank in the LEC?

Vladi (FNC, Mid), at Challenger · 4414 LP. It is the highest of the 51 LEC players that LoLProTrackr has indexed.

How many professional players does the LEC have?

50 players across 10 teams, with about 172 SoloQ accounts between them: an average of 3.4 accounts per player. It comes from counting the LEC leaderboard, not from an estimate.

Can you tell when a LEC player queues up for SoloQ?

Yes. LoLProTrackr posts to your Discord channel with the champion, the role, the rank and the team of the LEC player as soon as the game starts, without you having to ask for anything.

Which teams are in the LEC?

Fnatic (FNC), G2 Esports (G2), GIANTX (GX), Karmine Corp (KC), Movistar KOI (MKOI), Natus Vincere (NAVI), Shifters (SHFT), SK Gaming (SK), Team Heretics (TH), Team Vitality (VIT).

When is the next LEC match?

Team Vitality vs G2 Esports, on Saturday 5 September at 10:00 UTC (Playoffs). After that there are 7 more spread over 7 days of the schedule.

Where do the LEC SoloQ accounts come from?

From each player's known accounts, checked one by one against Riot's API on euw1. For every player the highest-ranked account is published, not all of them: someone with four accounts has a single relevant rank, the one they compete on.
