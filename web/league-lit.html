Frequently asked questions

Who has the highest SoloQ rank in the LIT?

Jekko (ZENA, ), at Challenger · 2588 LP. It is the highest of the 37 LIT players that LoLProTrackr has indexed.

How many professional players does the LIT have?

41 players across 8 teams, with about 141 SoloQ accounts between them: an average of 3.4 accounts per player. It comes from counting the LIT leaderboard, not from an estimate.

Can you tell when a LIT player queues up for SoloQ?

Yes. LoLProTrackr posts to your Discord channel with the champion, the role, the rank and the team of the LIT player as soon as the game starts, without you having to ask for anything.

Which teams are in the LIT?

CG (CG), EKO (EKO), GMBL (GMBL), G2 Esports (HMB), P11 (P11), TTS (TTS), Zena Esports (ZENA).

When is the next LIT match?

Colossal Gaming vs HMBLE, on Thursday 3 September at 16:00 UTC (Playoffs). After that there are 4 more spread over 5 days of the schedule.

Where do the LIT SoloQ accounts come from?

From each player's known accounts, checked one by one against Riot's API on euw1. For every player the highest-ranked account is published, not all of them: someone with four accounts has a single relevant rank, the one they compete on.
