Frequently asked questions

Who has the highest SoloQ rank in the MSI?

Caliste (KC, Bot), at Challenger · 3241 LP. It is the highest of the 58 MSI players that LoLProTrackr has indexed.

How many professional players does the MSI have?

57 players across 11 teams, with about 196 SoloQ accounts between them: an average of 3.4 accounts per player. It comes from counting the MSI leaderboard, not from an estimate.

Can you tell when a MSI player queues up for SoloQ?

Yes. LoLProTrackr posts to your Discord channel with the champion, the role, the rank and the team of the MSI player as soon as the game starts, without you having to ask for anything.

Which teams are in the MSI?

Bilibili Gaming (BLG), Deep Cross Gaming (DCG), FURIA (FUR), G2 Esports (G2), Hanwha Life Esports (HLE), Karmine Corp (KC), LYON (2024 American Team) (LYON), T1 (T1), Top Esports (TES), Team Liquid (TLAW), Team Secret Whales (TSW).

Which server do MSI players play on?

Several: the MSI (International) brings together players from different regions, so the server is resolved account by account instead of assuming one for the whole league. That is why a player in this league can show the rank of one server and play on another.
