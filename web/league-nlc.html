Frequently asked questions

Who has the highest SoloQ rank in the NLC?

Nan0 (RUD, Mid), at Challenger · 3630 LP. It is the highest of the 73 NLC players that LoLProTrackr has indexed.

How many professional players does the NLC have?

74 players across 15 teams, with about 255 SoloQ accounts between them: an average of 3.4 accounts per player. It comes from counting the NLC leaderboard, not from an estimate.

Can you tell when a NLC player queues up for SoloQ?

Yes. LoLProTrackr posts to your Discord channel with the champion, the role, the rank and the team of the NLC player as soon as the game starts, without you having to ask for anything.

When is the next NLC match?

Arctic Pandas vs Ruddy Corporation, on Monday 7 September at 16:00 UTC (Playoffs). After that there are 3 more spread over 4 days of the schedule.

Where do the NLC SoloQ accounts come from?

From each player's known accounts, checked one by one against Riot's API on euw1. For every player the highest-ranked account is published, not all of them: someone with four accounts has a single relevant rank, the one they compete on.
