Frequently asked questions

Who has the highest SoloQ rank in the CBLOL?

Kaze (LLL, Mid), at Challenger · 4052 LP. It is the highest of the 40 CBLOL players that LoLProTrackr has indexed.

How many professional players does the CBLOL have?

38 players across 8 teams, with about 131 SoloQ accounts between them: an average of 3.4 accounts per player. It comes from counting the CBLOL leaderboard, not from an estimate.

Can you tell when a CBLOL player queues up for SoloQ?

Yes. LoLProTrackr posts to your Discord channel with the champion, the role, the rank and the team of the CBLOL player as soon as the game starts, without you having to ask for anything.

Which teams are in the CBLOL?

FURIA (FUR), Fluxo W7M (FX), Leviatan (LEV), LOUD (LLL), LØS (LOS), paiN Gaming (PNG), RED Canids (RED), Vivo Keyd Stars (VKS).

When is the next CBLOL match?

Vivo Keyd Stars vs paiN Gaming, on Saturday 5 September at 16:00 UTC (Playoffs). After that there are 9 more spread over 10 days of the schedule.

Where do the CBLOL SoloQ accounts come from?

From each player's known accounts, checked one by one against Riot's API on br1. For every player the highest-ranked account is published, not all of them: someone with four accounts has a single relevant rank, the one they compete on.
