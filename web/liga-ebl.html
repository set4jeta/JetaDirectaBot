Frequently asked questions

Who has the highest SoloQ rank in the EBL?

Kozak (SPK, Bot), at Challenger · 2314 LP. It is the highest of the 29 EBL players that LoLProTrackr has indexed.

How many professional players does the EBL have?

29 players across 6 teams, with about 100 SoloQ accounts between them: an average of 3.4 accounts per player. It comes from counting the EBL leaderboard, not from an estimate.

Can you tell when a EBL player queues up for SoloQ?

Yes. LoLProTrackr posts to your Discord channel with the champion, the role, the rank and the team of the EBL player as soon as the game starts, without you having to ask for anything.

Which teams are in the EBL?

Hooligans (HLG), LUP (LUP), Magaza Esports (MGZ), RXCF (RXCF), SPIKE Syndicate (SPK), The Secret Club (TSC).

Where do the EBL SoloQ accounts come from?

From each player's known accounts, checked one by one against Riot's API on euw1. For every player the highest-ranked account is published, not all of them: someone with four accounts has a single relevant rank, the one they compete on.
