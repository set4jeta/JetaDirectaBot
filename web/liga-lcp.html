Frequently asked questions

Who has the highest SoloQ rank in the LCP?

Dire (TSW, Mid), at Challenger · 2280 LP. It is the highest of the 36 LCP players that LoLProTrackr has indexed.

How many professional players does the LCP have?

38 players across 8 teams, with about 131 SoloQ accounts between them: an average of 3.4 accounts per player. It comes from counting the LCP leaderboard, not from an estimate.

Can you tell when a LCP player queues up for SoloQ?

Yes. LoLProTrackr posts to your Discord channel with the champion, the role, the rank and the team of the LCP player as soon as the game starts, without you having to ask for anything.

Which teams are in the LCP?

CTBC Flying Oyster (CFO), Deep Cross Gaming (DCG), DetonatioN FocusMe (DFM), GAM Esports (GAM), Ground Zero Gaming (GZ), MVK Esports (MVK), Fukuoka SoftBank HAWKS gaming (SHG), Team Secret Whales (TSW).

Which server do LCP players play on?

Several: the LCP (Asia-Pacific) brings together players from different regions, so the server is resolved account by account instead of assuming one for the whole league. That is why a player in this league can show the rank of one server and play on another.
