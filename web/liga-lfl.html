Frequently asked questions

Who has the highest SoloQ rank in the LFL?

Nafkelah (SC, Mid), at Challenger · 3770 LP. It is the highest of the 46 LFL players that LoLProTrackr has indexed.

How many professional players does the LFL have?

52 players across 10 teams, with about 179 SoloQ accounts between them: an average of 3.4 accounts per player. It comes from counting the LFL leaderboard, not from an estimate.

Can you tell when a LFL player queues up for SoloQ?

Yes. LoLProTrackr posts to your Discord channel with the champion, the role, the rank and the team of the LFL player as soon as the game starts, without you having to ask for anything.

Which teams are in the LFL?

ES (ES), Galions (GL), Ici Japon Corp. Esport (IJC), KCB (KCB), Skillcamp (SC), Solary (SLY), TLN Pirates (TLNP), Vitality.Bee (VITB), ZYB Esport (ZYB).

Where do the LFL SoloQ accounts come from?

From each player's known accounts, checked one by one against Riot's API on euw1. For every player the highest-ranked account is published, not all of them: someone with four accounts has a single relevant rank, the one they compete on.
