Frequently asked questions

Who has the highest SoloQ rank in the LPL?

Kael (AL, Support), at Challenger · 2590 LP. It is the highest of the 61 LPL players that LoLProTrackr has indexed.

How many professional players does the LPL have?

62 players across 12 teams, with about 213 SoloQ accounts between them: an average of 3.4 accounts per player. It comes from counting the LPL leaderboard, not from an estimate.

Can you tell when a LPL player queues up for SoloQ?

Not live. The LPL plays on servers that Riot's API does not expose, so ranks and standings can be read, but the game in progress cannot be detected.

Which teams are in the LPL?

Anyone's Legend (AL), Bilibili Gaming (BLG), EDward Gaming (EDG), Invictus Gaming (IG), JD Gaming (JDG), LGD Gaming (LGD), LNG Esports (LNG), Ninjas in Pyjamas.CN (NIP), Top Esports (TES), ThunderTalk Gaming (TT), Weibo Gaming (WBG), Team WE (WE).

When is the next LPL match?

LGD GAMING vs Anyone's Legend, on Friday 4 September at 09:00 UTC (Playoffs). After that there are 11 more spread over 10 days of the schedule.

Why are LPL games not detected live?

Because the LPL is played on servers that Riot's public API does not expose. Rank and standings can be read, but the current-game query returns nothing for those accounts, so alerting on a live game is not possible without making the data up.
