Frequently asked questions

Who has the highest SoloQ rank in the Rift Legends?

Mrozku (BOOM, Mid), at Challenger · 3957 LP. It is the highest of the 35 Rift Legends players that LoLProTrackr has indexed.

How many professional players does the Rift Legends have?

35 players across 7 teams, with about 120 SoloQ accounts between them: an average of 3.4 accounts per player. It comes from counting the Rift Legends leaderboard, not from an estimate.

Can you tell when a Rift Legends player queues up for SoloQ?

Yes. LoLProTrackr posts to your Discord channel with the champion, the role, the rank and the team of the Rift Legends player as soon as the game starts, without you having to ask for anything.

Which teams are in the Rift Legends?

Anonymo Esports (ANO), Barcząca Esports by Yumisu (BCE), BOOM (BOOM), DOCISK (DOC), devils.one inStreamly (DV1), Forsaken (Polish Team) (FSK), LODIS (Polish Team) (LDS).

When is the next Rift Legends match?

Barcząca Esports vs DOCISK is being played right now. The official schedule still lists 7 Rift Legends matches.

Where do the Rift Legends SoloQ accounts come from?

From each player's known accounts, checked one by one against Riot's API on euw1. For every player the highest-ranked account is published, not all of them: someone with four accounts has a single relevant rank, the one they compete on.
